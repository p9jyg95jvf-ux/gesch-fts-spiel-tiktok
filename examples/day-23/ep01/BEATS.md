## The plan in one paragraph
You wake in a dim hospital room with something rattling the door. The calendar says you slept 23 days, and nobody came.
The hallway is empty; at the end, chained doors bow as the dead push from the other side. A dog barks from a side
doorway and leads you down the stairs and out onto Main Street, which is empty. Almost: one figure stands far up the
road. It turns. Then all of them turn. Vote: run for the police car, hide in the bus, or go back inside. Big answer:
the world ended while you slept. New hook: the dead have seen you, so where do you go?
