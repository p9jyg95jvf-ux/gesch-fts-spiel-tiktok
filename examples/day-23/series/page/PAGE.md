## Setting it up (in the Instagram app)
1. Sign up with a new email (or "Add account" from your profile). Username `day23.series`; name as above.
2. Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account → Creator → Video creator. Don't sync contacts;
   follow no one yet.
3. Edit profile: the picture `avatar_A_23.png`, the bio above, "Display category" on.
4. Settings → Media quality → turn on **Upload at highest quality**.
5. Comments open to everyone; story replies from everyone (the votes and the clue hunt live there).
