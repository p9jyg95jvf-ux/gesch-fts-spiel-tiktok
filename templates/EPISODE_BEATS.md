## Fun check (answer before approval)
- [ ] Frame 0 is mid-action with sound; no title card, no recap.
- [ ] A new picture every 1.5-2.5 s; nothing held over 3.5 s before the vote; every shot moves (push, drift, whip).
- [ ] Something happens every ~3 s; a real story turn every ~7 s; the threat changes TYPE about every 20 s.
- [ ] Narration: 4-5 words per sentence on average, 10 at most; every line names something on screen NOW; at most 3
      explaining lines, each paid off by action within 5 s; countdown triplets, antithesis pairs, one-word hits.
- [ ] The companion makes visible choices; its face shows its feelings in every shot it's in.
- [ ] One clip-able spectacle around 60-65% of the runtime, with a pause-bait detail hidden in it (for the pinned comment).
- [ ] Every twist pays off something the viewer already saw (fair surprise, never random).
- [ ] Lore only as the consequence of an action, spoken over the object it's about. No characters explaining backstory.
- [ ] The cliffhanger is big, bright and on screen long enough to read. A sound cuts off on the last frame.
- [ ] No spoilers in what the title/cover will show.
