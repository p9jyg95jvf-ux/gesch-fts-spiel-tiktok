## 1. The promise (what the viewer gets, every episode)

- **Premise in one line:** _you wake from a coma on day 23 of the end of the world, and a stray dog knows the way out_.
- **Who "you" are:** the viewer IS the hero, told in the second person, present tense ("You wake up. Something is at the door.").
- **The loop:** every episode ends on a 2-3 option vote; the most-voted option is what happens next. Say it plainly in the
  caption of every post: "The votes decide Episode N+1."
- **The feel:** _cosy mystery with real stakes_ (pick 2-3 words: they steer every choice of look, voice and sound).

## 6. Vote rules

- Two options, three at most. Each is a physical verb about what's in the last frame, each with a felt stake.
- Teach before you vote: every option must already make sense from what the episode showed.
- Aim for a near-even split (55/45 is a great vote; 90/10 means one option was a trap).
- Never "going home" / ending the story as an option.

## 8. Look and sound

- **Palette:** _sick green-grey inside, a dusty washed-out street outside; one strong red (the dog's bandana)._
- **Colour script per place:** _hospital = threat, street = golden (desaturated), night = night, memory = memory (ONE flashback look only)._
- **Home setups (4-5 camera setups the episodes return to):** _1 your room wide; 2 the long hallway; 3 the street from the middle of the road; 4 the companion close..._
- **Voices:** narrator + one voice per speaking character (series/VOICES.json). Same voices every episode.
- **Sound:** _hum indoors, wind and moans outdoors; every hit on a word or a picture moment; a sound cut off on the last frame._
