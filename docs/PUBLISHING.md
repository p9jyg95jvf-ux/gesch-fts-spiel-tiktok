# Publishing and growing a vote-driven series

Claude prepares everything (files, covers, title, captions, pinned comments, a checklist); **the creator posts.**
Claude never posts, never DMs, never creates accounts and never spends money without the creator's OK.

## What the format looks like on short-video platforms (late 2026)

- Faceless 2D "you" stories with very simple characters do well: a hook in under 3 s, a HUD (day, time, place), numbered
  RULE cards that become chips, a resource meter that shrinks, a twist, and a moral-choice vote with 2 pill buttons,
  then a series card ("DAY 1 → DAY 2 / FOLLOW FOR PART 2").
- **Episode 1 is always the biggest post.** Views drop to about 30-50% by episode 2. Followers arrive at roughly 1-4% of
  episode 1's views.
- **Vote endings earn 5-15x more comments.** Instagram's Reel POLL pulls far more votes than comments: always add it.
- The pages that break out post often (some post 2-3 parts a day). One breakout lifts every later part, so have the next
  2-3 episodes in the pipeline. Use the same cover style every episode.
- Shares (sends) are the strongest signal for reaching new people; hidden clues and pause-bait get sent.

## The vote rule (wording matters)

Ask for the vote NUMBER in only two places: the pills in the video and ONE pinned comment. Captions ask the story
question in words. Platforms may show posts less to new people when the caption asks people to comment "a specific
word, number or emoji". Never: "Comment 1 to unlock part 2", "Share or the dog dies".

## Every episode, in order

1. **YouTube Short first** (about an hour before Instagram): title, description with links to every earlier episode
   in order, Not made for kids, Film & Animation, the cover as thumbnail; wait for "Checks complete" before Publish.
2. **Instagram Reel:** the caption (story question in words), the Reel poll, the cover; "Link a reel" from the
   previous episode to this one, and from this one back to Episode 1.
3. **Pin the vote comment** on both. Votes stay open at least 24 h, including a full US daytime.
4. **Pin a clue comment** ("There's something hidden at 0:21 👀"). If nobody finds it in ~12 h, post a hint.
5. **Story** (Instagram): share the reel with a poll sticker, no spoilers.
6. **Reply** to 10-15 good comments in the first hour and a few each day after (the first 1-2 days carry most of the
   reach). Never confirm a theory: pin a great guess with "We're not saying anything 👀".
7. **After 24 h:** count the vote (pinned comment + poll), write it into the next episode's `stats.json`, update
   `series/CANON.md`, then start the next episode from the winner.

## Covers and titles

- Spoiler-free: the situation, never the twist, never the answer, never the vote numbers.
- The hero's face big and readable at grid size; the title in the top third; keep everything inside the 3:4 grid crop
  (y 240-1680). `arclab covers` renders options from frames you pick and shows the grid crop.
- The same title style every episode, so the profile grid reads as one series.

## Don't

- Buy followers or likes, join engagement pods, follow/unfollow, run giveaways, or use auto-DM tools.
- Promise a posting time you might miss. If late: one Story with the new time; never go silent.
- Re-upload episodes stitched together, or send people to another app mid-story.
- Ask fans for real names, ages, selfies or locations; strip those from any fan text you feature.
- Use anyone else's characters, music, logos or footage. Original series only: that's what you can grow and earn from.
